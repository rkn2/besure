Building Engineering — Seminal Undergraduate Research Experience

BE-SURE connects undergraduate students with faculty mentors for paid research positions in Architectural Engineering. Explore real-world problems, develop research skills, and present your work to the broader community.

About the Program

Get paid to do real research

The BE-SURE program pairs undergraduates with faculty mentors for hands-on research experience. The position pays $15/hour with an average commitment of about 5 hours per week, though some students work up to 10. You and your mentor decide the schedule that works for your project.

All student researchers participate in a poster session at the end of each year (April) to present their progress and accomplishments. The program also connects you with information about graduate school fellowships, research scholarships, and professional development opportunities through our Microsoft Teams channel.

Research Areas

Four areas of Architectural Engineering

When you apply, you'll rate your interest in each area. We use these to match you with faculty whose research aligns with your interests.

Structures

Structural optimization, digital fabrication, historic preservation, generative design, and conservation of built heritage.

Mechanical / Energy

Smart building controls, energy modeling, HVAC optimization, indoor air quality, and human-building interaction.

Lighting / Electrical

Building envelopes, smart facades, daylighting, lighting science, color science, and health impacts of lighting.

Construction

Virtual reality, digital twins, robotic construction, integrated project delivery, sustainable concretes, and automation.
