Welcome to AE Research Scholars, Fall 2026!

Congratulations on being placed with a faculty mentor this semester! You're joining a community of undergraduate researchers across Architectural Engineering.

Before you start

Please make sure you are officially hired in Workday and have completed your background check before you begin logging hours. If you run into any issues with the hiring process, reach out to Latrisha and she can direct you to the right people.

What you'll be doing

You'll work directly with your faculty mentor on their active research. You and your mentor will set a schedule that works for both of you. Use this semester to dig into the work, ask questions, and start building expertise in your area.

Poster session in the spring

All AE Research Scholars present their work at our poster session in April. This is a great opportunity to practice communicating your research and to see what everyone else has been working on. We'll share more details as it gets closer, but keep it on your radar—you'll want to start thinking about your poster as your work progresses.
